What we collect

  • Account details: email address, display name, and a one-way hash of your password.

Why we use it

Only to provide the service: calculating your chart, checking it against your past, and answering your questions. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party AI providers (optional)

To answer your questions, Jyotish AI sends your chart summary and recent messages to external AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter). By accepting, you agree to this processing as part of the service. External AI replies are included in the service terms accepted at signup. Existing accounts are asked to accept the updated terms before continuing with AI chat. This is not a separate optional setting.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in our database ([HOSTING PROVIDER, REGION]) for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your account, birth details, calibration answers and conversations.

Your rights

  • Download everything we hold about you: chat menu → “Download my data”.
  • You may stop using AI chat at any time. If you no longer agree to external AI processing, you can download your data and delete your account from the chat menu.
  • Delete your account: chat menu → “Delete account”.
